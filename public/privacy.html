Konvene

Privacy

Short version: your call goes straight between the people in it. Effects run on your own device. Nothing is stored on a server, and there is no account.

Camera and microphone

Your camera and microphone are off until you turn them on. The home page never uses them. Before every call you see a preview page where you choose your camera and microphone and whether they are on, and nobody sees or hears you until you press Join. Next time, the preview page starts with the choices you made last time. Konvene needs a microphone to join, but you can stay muted.

Video and voice

Calls use WebRTC, so video and voice travel directly between the browsers in the room, encrypted. We don't run a media server, so we never receive or store your call.

Screen sharing and chat

A screen you share and the chat messages you send travel the same way as video: directly to the other people in the call. Chat messages are not saved anywhere and disappear when you leave. Your browser asks you which screen, window or tab to share, and you can stop at any time.

Effects

Background blur, virtual backgrounds, looks, lighting, face touch-ups, makeup, face warps and expression effects are calculated in your browser using Google MediaPipe models that are downloaded from this site. They change the picture before it is sent. The face tracking finds points on your face to place effects; it does not recognise who you are. A background image you upload stays on your device.

Smart features

Pausing your video when you step away, keeping you in frame, fixing low light and the reminder when you talk while muted all work on your device, using the same face tracking and microphone level as the rest of the app. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Call links

A call link looks like konvene.example/abc-def-ghj. The website host sees which call code a browser opened, as it does for any web address, but it is never part of the call and cannot see or hear it.

How people find each other

To connect, browsers exchange a short handshake through public Nostr relays, and use public STUN servers (Google and Cloudflare) to find a direct route. These services, and the other people in the call, can see your IP address, as with any video call. They do not receive your video or voice.

Some networks (strict offices, hotels, some mobile carriers) block direct connections. If whoever runs this site has set up a relay (a TURN server, for example Cloudflare's), your call is passed through it on those networks only. The relay forwards your call still encrypted and cannot see or hear it. It can see your IP address. To get a short-lived relay password, your browser asks this site's /api/turn address when the page opens.

Recording

Anyone in a call can record it in their own browser. Everyone sees a "recording" badge with that person's name while it runs. The file is created on the recorder's device and is only saved if they choose to. In many countries you need everyone's consent to record a call, so please ask first.

What this site stores

Your browser remembers your name, your effect choices, which camera, microphone and speakers you picked and whether you had them on, in local storage on your device. No cookies, no analytics, no ads.
