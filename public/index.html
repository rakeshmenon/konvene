Only the people in your call can see or hear it.

Most video apps send your call through their servers, where it can be decoded. Konvene connects browsers directly and encrypts everything between them. A few services help the call start, and on some networks one passes it along, but none of them can see or hear it.

Who else helps, and what they can see

This website
Sends your browser the Konvene app from Cloudflare's network. It sees which call link was opened, never the call itself.
Connection helpers
Public Nostr relays pass along a scrambled handshake, and STUN servers from Google and Cloudflare tell your browser its own address. They see your IP address, not your call.
A relay, on some networks
Strict offices, hotels and some mobile networks block direct calls. There, the call passes through a relay run by Cloudflare, still encrypted. It can't see or hear it.

As on any video call, the people you talk to can see your IP address too. Read the privacy details, with exactly what each service handles.

Questions

Is Konvene really free?

Yes. There's no paid plan and no ads. Calls go straight between browsers, so they cost almost nothing to run.

Can anyone else see or hear my call?

Only the people in it. Video, voice, screens and chat go directly between your browsers, encrypted. On some strict networks the call is passed through a relay, still encrypted, and the relay can't see or hear it.

Are any servers involved?

Yes, but none of them carry your call in a form they can read. This website sends you the app, public relays help browsers find each other, and on some strict networks a relay passes the call along, still encrypted. The privacy page lists each one and what it can see.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. Open the link, type a name and join. Konvene works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, on computers and phones.

How many people can join?

Up to {{MAX}}. Each browser sends its video to each other person, which keeps the call private and limits its size.

What do people in the call learn about me?

The name you type, and, as on any video call, your IP address.

Can I run my own copy?

Yes. Konvene is open source under the AGPL. Deploy it to Netlify or Vercel with one click, or put it on any web host. How to run your own
