Video calls that stay between you.

Konvene connects you straight to the people you call. No account, no app to install, and no server in the middle keeping a copy. Free, in your browser.

Private by design, not by policy.

Most video apps promise to look after your data. Konvene is built so there is nothing to look after.

Straight to them
Video, voice, screens and messages travel directly between your browsers, encrypted the whole way.
Nothing kept
No server records your call or your chat. Messages disappear when you leave, and recordings stay on the device that made them.
No account
No email, phone number or password. Type a name and start talking.
No tracking
No cookies, no analytics, no ads. Nothing about you is sold because nothing about you is collected.
Your face stays home
Background blur, lighting and effects are worked out on your own device. Your camera is never uploaded to be processed.
Recording is never secret
Recording asks first and shows everyone a badge with the name of the person recording.

Being straight with you: as on any video call, the people you talk to and the public services that help browsers find each other can see your IP address. Read the privacy details

Smart, without the cloud.

Features that usually need someone else's servers, done on your own device.

Steps away when you do
Leave your desk and your video pauses with a "Stepped away" card. Sit back down and you're live again.
Keeps you in frame
Lean back or shift around and the picture gently follows you.
Fixes a dark room
Calling from a dim room? Your video brightens on its own.
Notices when you're muted
Start talking with your mic off and Konvene tells you, before anyone else has to.
Knows you from your chair
Background blur that follows your hair and shoulders and leaves the furniture out.

Plus face touch-up and makeup, background blur and virtual backgrounds, screen sharing, chat, reactions, raise hand and recording, for up to six people.

Open source, all of it.

Every promise on this page is in code anyone can read. Run Konvene on your own domain, change it, make it yours.
