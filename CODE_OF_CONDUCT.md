# Code of Conduct

Konvene is a project for people who want to talk to each other privately and kindly. We want working on it to feel the same way.

## Our standards

We expect everyone in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space to:

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with newcomers and people whose first language isn't English.
- Assume good intent, and give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.
- Own mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information (including their address, email or face) without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or encouraging any of the above.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, Rakesh Menon, through his GitHub profile (github.com/rakeshmenon), or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue. Reports are handled privately and as quickly as we can.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
